IS25LP064A_efb 1.0.0

APB-mapped access block for an ISSI IS25LP064A (64 Mbit) serial flash on the MachXO4 EFB SPI controller. The block is placed between the APB interconnect and the lscc_efb IP (APB interface, 32-bit data, 10-bit address) and passes every APB signal through 1:1. It has no registers and no clock; it gives the flash its own address window and comes with a BSP driver.

APB interconnect --APB_S0--> IS25LP064A_efb --APB_M0--> lscc_efb APB_S0 --SPI--> IS25LP064A

lscc_efb settings required

  • Interface APB, APB data width 32, SPI enabled (controller mode), 1 chip select.
  • .pdc: ldc_set_sysconfig {MASTER_SPI_PORT=EFB_USER}, otherwise the dual-purpose SPI pins (MCLK, SISPI, SPISO, CSSPIN) are released as GPIO and the controller never receives data.
  • Flash WP# and HOLD# held high (board pull-ups or GPIO).

BSP driver (IS25LP064A_efb.h)

  • Set-up: is25lp064a_efb_init, is25lp064a_set_sck, is25lp064a_probe
  • Identification and registers: read_jedec_id, read_unique_id, read_status, read_function, write_status, write_enable, write_disable, wait_ready, is_busy
  • Protection: set_protection, is_protected
  • Read: read, fast_read, read_block, blank_check
  • Program: program_page, program (page split), write_block (erase, program, verify)
  • Erase: erase_block / erase_sector (4 KB), erase_32k, erase_64k, erase_chip
  • Power and reset: software_reset, deep_power_down, release_power_down
  • Raw: transfer, efb_spisr, strerror

A "block" in read_block, write_block and erase_block is one 4 KB sector (2048 blocks). All functions are blocking, return 0 or a negative IS25LP064A_ERR_* code, and use no interrupts.
